Privacy Policy & Child Data Safeguards

Talent Hunt, created by Abhijit Kumar Misra, is built on trust, transparency, and child safety. We are committed to safeguarding the personal data of young learners and their families.

1. Platform Commitment to Child Privacy

Talent Hunt is an educational and Olympiad preparatory platform primarily serving Class III students (ages 7 to 9) and their guardians. We adhere strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) standards.

Our platform is completely ad-free. We do not sell, rent, license, or trade student personal information to commercial advertising networks or third-party marketing companies under any circumstances.

2. Collection of Personal Information (Item 2a)

We practice strict data minimization. We only collect the minimal information necessary to deliver educational learning and password recovery:

  • Student Display Name & Avatar: A friendly screen name and child-friendly cartoon avatar for leaderboards and quiz scorecards.
  • Grade Level & Educational Progress: Academic class (Class III), XP score points, earned star badges, and quiz attempt histories.
  • Guardian / Parent Email: Provided optionally during registration for account recovery, certificate delivery, and score report updates.
  • Security Verification Questions: Favorite city and pet answers collected optionally to enable self-service password recovery without requiring external tracking.

What We Never Collect: We never collect biometric identifiers, student phone numbers, live GPS geolocation data, or social media graphs.

3. Protection of Personal Information (Item 2b)

We deploy defense-in-depth technical and operational safeguards to secure learner data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure:

  • Bcrypt Salted Hashing: All account passwords and security verification answers are encrypted using industry-standard bcrypt hashes with salt rounds. Plaintext answers are never stored.
  • Stateless JWT Tokens: User sessions are authenticated using cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT) signed with high-entropy secrets that meet strict production security validations.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between user devices and Talent Hunt servers is encrypted via TLS/HTTPS protocols.
  • Automated Rate Limiting: Sliding-window rate limiters defend authentication and password recovery endpoints against brute-force attacks and credential stuffing.

4. How Long Information Is Retained (Item 2c)

Student learning records, star counts, and quiz attempts are retained only for the duration of the active academic session or as long as the user maintains an active learning account.

  • Right to Complete Erasure / Forgotten: Parents, guardians, or students may submit a data deletion or cancellation request at any time by emailing abhijit.jitu4u@gmail.com. Upon receipt and verification, all account credentials, personal identifiers, and quiz histories are permanently erased from all production databases within 72 business hours.
  • Guest Sessions: Guest student mode operates via ephemeral client-side storage with zero persistent identity retention on the backend server.

5. Payment Provider Privacy & Transaction Security

Talent Hunt does not collect, capture, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, CVVs, net banking credentials, or UPI PINs on its servers. All payments for Amazon KDP books are handled directly on Amazon's PCI-DSS compliant infrastructure, and any direct course mock packages are processed strictly via certified, authorized payment gateways complying with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) norms.

6. Privacy Officer & Support Contact (Item 4)

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, parental consent verification, or data erasure requests, please contact the developer and administrator directly:

Sutra 1 ⚡

Ekadhikena Purvena

"By One More Than the Previous One"

Key Application: Instant Squaring of any number ending in 5 in under 2 seconds!
35² = (3 × 4) | 25 = 1225
75² = (7 × 8) | 25 = 5625
Sutra 2 ⚡

Nikhilam Navatashcaramam Dashatah

"All from 9 and the Last from 10"

Key Application: Lightning multiplication of numbers near bases (10, 100, 1000).
97 × 94 → (-3, -6)
(97 - 6) | (3 × 6) = 9118
Sutra 3 ⚡

Urdhva-Tiryagbhyam

"Vertically and Crosswise"

Key Application: Universal formula for multiplying 2-digit & 3-digit numbers in one mental line.
23 × 12 → (2×1)|(2×2+3×1)|(3×2)
= 2 | 7 | 6 = 276
Sutra 4 ⚡

Paravartya Yojayet

"Transpose and Apply"

Key Application: Instant algebraic equation solving and rapid polynomial division without paper.
ax + b = cx + d
x = (d - b) / (a - c) in one glance!
